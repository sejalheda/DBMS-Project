Why CivicConnect?

Empowering citizens and local authorities with intelligent grievance resolution

📍

Location-Based Complaints

Citizens can provide their current location using browser geolocation or enter coordinates manually.

🔎

Duplicate Detection

Similar complaints from nearby locations are automatically grouped to avoid repeated issue entries.

🤖

AI Priority Prediction

A machine learning model predicts complaint priority using complaint information and severity.

🏢

Automatic Department Assignment

Complaints are automatically mapped to the responsible civic department based on category.

📊

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can view consolidated issues, priorities, locations and update complaint status.

📱

Complaint Tracking

Citizens can log in and view the real-time status of their own submitted complaints.

How CivicConnect Works

A simple 5-step process from report to resolution

1

Register

Citizen submits a complaint issue with details.

2

Locate

Complaint location coordinates are captured.

3

Analyze

Duplicate and priority analysis is performed.

4

Assign

Complaint is mapped to responsible department.

5

Track

Citizen and admin track resolution progress.
